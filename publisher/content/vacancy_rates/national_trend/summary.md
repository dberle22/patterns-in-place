# Summary — National Vacancy Rate Trend

The US housing vacancy rate was essentially flat for most of the 2010s, holding
between 12.1% and 12.5% from 2012 through 2019. Since then it has fallen every
year, dropping from 12.1% in 2019 to 10.1% in 2024, a 2.0 percentage point
decline that points to a much tighter national housing market.

The housing-unit-weighted metro average also declined over the same period,
falling from 11.3% in 2019 to 9.4% in 2024. That reinforces the broader story:
housing slack has been shrinking across the country, not just in a handful of
markets.
