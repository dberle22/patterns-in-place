**Written:** before EDA  
**Status:** done

## The Question

How has national vacancy rates changed since 2019?

## The Angle

Housing supply has been tightening and costs have risen dramatically, you would expect to see this born out in the national vacancy rate change.

## Visual Hypothesis

I expect this data in a line chart format so we can see change over time. We should include the US Average and US Metro Average so we there's a little more depth.

## Audience

Both X and Substack.

## What Would Change the Angle

If Vacancy rates have gone up or have not changed meaningfully.
