# EDA 02 Summary

## What this checked

The full US vacancy-rate series from 2012 through 2024, including annual change
in percentage points.

## Key numbers

- 2012-2014: 12.5%
- 2015: 12.3%
- 2016: 12.2%
- 2017-2018: 12.2%
- 2019: 12.1%
- 2020: 11.6%
- 2021: 11.2%
- 2022: 10.8%
- 2023: 10.4%
- 2024: 10.1%

## Takeaways

- The series is basically flat from 2012 through 2019, staying in a narrow
  12.1%-12.5% band.
- The tightening starts in earnest after 2019. Every year from 2020 through
  2024 is lower than the year before.
- The five-year change is large enough to be post-worthy: 12.1% in 2019 to
  10.1% in 2024, a `-2.0pp` drop.
- The biggest one-year drop is 2019 to 2020 (`-0.5pp`), followed by a steady
  run of `-0.4pp`, `-0.4pp`, `-0.4pp`, and `-0.3pp`.

## Why it matters for this insight

- The data clearly supports the national-hook angle in `question.md`.
- A single US line is enough to tell the headline story cleanly.
