# EDA 03 Summary

## What this checked

How much each region changed after `2019`, whether the regional spread narrowed
or widened, and whether the rank order of regions changed over time.

## Takeaways

- The `South` posted the biggest `2019`-to-`2024` decline at `-2.3pp`, followed
  by the `Northeast` (`-2.2pp`), `Midwest` (`-2.0pp`), and `West` (`-1.5pp`).
- Even with all four regions tightening, the spread did not disappear.
  The gap between the tightest and loosest regions was `4.0pp` in `2019` and
  still `3.1pp` in `2024`.
- The broad rank order is very stable. `West` is the tightest region in every
  year from `2012` through `2024`, and `South` is the loosest in every year.
- The only meaningful internal shuffle is that `Northeast` was slightly tighter
  than `Midwest` through `2015`, after which `Midwest` moved ahead and stayed
  tighter through `2024`.

## Why it matters for this insight

- The best message is "shared tightening with persistent regional differences,"
  not full convergence.
- A line chart should make both patterns legible:
  synchronized post-2019 declines and a largely stable regional hierarchy.
