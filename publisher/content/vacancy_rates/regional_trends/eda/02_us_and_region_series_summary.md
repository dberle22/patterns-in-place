# EDA 02 Summary

## What this checked

The full US and regional time series, with special attention to the
`2019`-to-`2024` comparison window in the brief.

## Takeaways

- All four regions declined from `2019` to `2024`:
  `South` fell from `14.0%` to `11.7%` (`-2.3pp`),
  `Northeast` from `11.8%` to `9.6%` (`-2.2pp`),
  `Midwest` from `11.4%` to `9.4%` (`-2.0pp`),
  and `West` from `10.0%` to `8.5%` (`-1.5pp`).
- The 2024 ordering is clear and intuitive:
  `West` (`8.5%`) is tightest, then `Midwest` (`9.4%`),
  `Northeast` (`9.6%`), and `South` (`11.7%`), versus `10.1%` for the US.
- The West sits below the US in every year of the `2019`-`2024` window,
  averaging about `-1.9pp` below the national line.
- The South sits above the US in every year of the same window,
  averaging about `+1.7pp` above the national line.

## Why it matters for this insight

- The chart should work well as a five-line comparison:
  one national reference line plus four regional lines.
- The strongest editorial framing is not just that all regions declined, but
  that they declined while preserving a clear regional hierarchy.
