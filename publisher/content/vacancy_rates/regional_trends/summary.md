# Summary — Regional Vacancy Trends

Every Census region saw housing vacancy fall from 2019 to 2024, reinforcing
the broader national tightening story. The biggest declines came in the
`South`, where vacancy fell from `14.0%` to `11.7%`, and the `Northeast`,
which dropped from `11.8%` to `9.6%`.

But the regional hierarchy barely changed. The `West` remained the tightest
region throughout the period, falling from `10.7%` in 2014 to `8.5%` in 2024,
while the `South` stayed the loosest even after its larger decline. That means
the US housing market is tightening across the board, but longstanding regional
differences in housing slack are still very much intact.
