# Question — State Maps

**Written:** before EDA  
**Status:** done

---

## The Question

Which states have the tightest housing markets — and which have tightened the most since 2019?  

## The Angle

Housing supplies have been tightening, but this is not equal across all states. We want to see how vacancy rates differ across states, and how they have changed over time.

## Visual Hypothesis

I expect this data in a national Choropleth Chart. Only including the lower 48 states. I want the color of the states to relate to the vacancy rate. I also want to visualize how the state's vacancy rate has changed by showing the 2019 rate under the current 2024 rate. 

## Audience

Both X and Substack.

## What Would Change the Angle

If Vacancy Rates do not change between states.
