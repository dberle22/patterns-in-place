## The Question

Which major metros have the tightest and loosest housing markets in 2024?  

## The Angle

There's this idea that coastal, liberal states have tighter housing markets because they don't have enough supply. 

## Visual Hypothesis

I expect bar charts so we can see individual markets. We should include both the tighest markets and the loosest, so we should show the national average then the 15 lowest and 15 highest vacancy rates. We can split it into multiple charts if needed.

## Audience

Both X and Substack.

## What Would Change the Angle

If there's no pattern to the top and bottom vacany rate markets.
