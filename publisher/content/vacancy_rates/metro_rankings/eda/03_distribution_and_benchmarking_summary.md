# EDA 03 Summary

## What this checked

The distribution of 2024 vacancy rates across major metros and how that
distribution compares with the national vacancy benchmark.

## Takeaways

- Among the `197` major metros, the median 2024 vacancy rate is `8.0%` and the
  average is `9.5%`, versus a `10.1%` US vacancy rate.
- `138` of the `197` major metros are tighter than the national rate, leaving
  only `59` at or above the US benchmark.
- The tight half of the distribution is compact:
  the 10th percentile is `5.1%` and the 25th percentile is `6.4%`.
- The loose half stretches much farther:
  the 75th percentile is `11.2%`, the 90th percentile is `14.2%`, and the max
  is `33.4%`.
- A housing-unit-weighted rollup of major-metro vacancy rates is `9.4%`, also
  below the true US rate.

## Why it matters for this insight

- Large metros are, on average, tighter than the national housing market in
  2024.
- The distribution is asymmetric, which argues for a chart that includes a US
  reference line or explicitly contrasts the tight and loose ends rather than
  implying a symmetric ranking spread.
