# EDA 04 Summary

## What this checked

The highest-vacancy CBSAs excluded by the `250k+` population screen.

## Takeaways

- The excluded outliers are exactly the kinds of markets we would expect to
  distort a broad metro ranking: `Nantucket, MA` (`58.3%`), `Ocean Pines, MD`
  (`57.6%`), `Vineyard Haven, MA` (`57.4%`), and `Breckenridge, CO` (`57.0%`).
- These are very small markets, mostly well below `100k` population, and many
  are obvious seasonal or second-home destinations.
- Even after excluding them, the major-metro loose tail is still real, but the
  chart no longer gets dominated by niche resort-market vacancy dynamics.

## Why it matters for this insight

- This validates the population filter in the brief.
- The cutoff is not arbitrary cleanup; it meaningfully separates mainstream
  metro housing markets from vacation-market vacancy patterns.
