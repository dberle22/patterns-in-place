# EDA 02 Summary

## What this checked

The actual ranking tables for major metros in 2024, using the `250k+`
population cutoff from the brief.

## Takeaways

- The tightest major metro is `Lancaster, PA` at `3.0%`, followed by
  `Provo-Orem-Lehi, UT` (`3.8%`) and `Modesto, CA` (`3.9%`).
- The rest of the tight end is compressed into a narrow range:
  the top 15 runs only from `3.0%` to `4.9%`, and the 20th-tightest major metro
  is still just `5.1%` (`Yakima, WA`).
- The loosest end is much more extreme. `Atlantic City-Hammonton, NJ` leads at
  `33.4%`, followed by `Naples-Marco Island, FL` (`30.7%`) and `Seaford, DE`
  (`29.9%`).
- The loose tail is spread out far more than the tight tail:
  the 20th-loosest major metro is `New Orleans-Metairie, LA` at `14.3%`, which
  is nearly three times the 20th-tightest rate.

## Why it matters for this insight

- A single "top 20 tightest metros" chart is valid, but it will show a fairly
  compressed cluster.
- The more visually dramatic story may be to pair the tightest and loosest
  metros, because the loose end contains much larger dispersion and more obvious
  outliers.
