# Summary — Metro Vacancy Rankings

Among major metros in the 50 states and DC, the tightest housing markets in
2024 were clustered in a narrow band: the top 20 ran from `3.0%` in
`Lancaster, PA` to just `5.1%` in `Yakima, WA`, all well below the US vacancy
rate of `10.1%`. That makes the tight side of the market look broad and fairly
compressed rather than dominated by a single region.

The loose side is much more extreme. The 20 loosest major metros ranged from
`13.9%` in `Palm Bay-Melbourne-Titusville, FL` to `33.4%` in
`Atlantic City-Hammonton, NJ`, with a heavy concentration in Florida and other
seasonal or tourism-oriented markets. Taken together, the three charts show a
national market where low vacancy is common across large metros, but very high
vacancy is concentrated in a smaller and more distinctive set of places.
