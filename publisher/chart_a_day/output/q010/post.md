Median age across major US metros is more concentrated than a lot of people might expect.

Most large metros cluster in the upper-30s, but the full range still runs from the mid-20s to the low-50s.

That makes age structure a useful second lens for housing and labor stories:
- younger metros often overlap with faster growth
- older metros can carry very different demand and service needs

Source: `gold.population_demographics` + `gold.dim_geo`
