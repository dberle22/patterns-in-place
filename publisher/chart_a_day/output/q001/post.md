## Standalone
Miami had the highest rent-to-income ratio among large US metros in 2023 at about 28.9%, followed by Cape Coral and Orlando. The list leans heavily toward Florida and coastal California, which is a strong signal that high rent pressure is not isolated to one regional growth story.

## Thread opener
Miami had the highest rent-to-income ratio among large US metros in 2023, at about 28.9%.

Cape Coral and Orlando were close behind, and the broader top 15 is packed with Florida and coastal California metros. That is a useful reminder that affordability pressure is clustering in both high-growth Sun Belt markets and long-expensive coastal ones.
