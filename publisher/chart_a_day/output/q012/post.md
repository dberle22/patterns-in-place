Phoenix is expensive, but its median gross rent is still a bit below the big-metro Western average.

For 2023:
- Phoenix: about `$1,572`
- Western-region benchmark: about `$1,659`

That’s a useful reminder that Phoenix feels expensive partly because it climbed fast, not because it’s the very highest-rent metro in the West.

Source: `gold.housing_core_wide` + `gold.dim_geo`
