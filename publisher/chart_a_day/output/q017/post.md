## Standalone
Rent burden barely improved in the biggest metros between 2018 and 2023. A few places like Chicago, Washington, and New York edged lower, but most large metros were flat to worse by 2023.

## Thread opener
Large-metro affordability did not meaningfully reset between 2018 and 2023.

Among the 15 biggest US metros, only a small handful posted lower rent-to-income ratios in 2023 than in 2018. Chicago, Washington, and New York improved a bit, but the broader pattern is stagnation or further pressure rather than a real affordability unwind.
