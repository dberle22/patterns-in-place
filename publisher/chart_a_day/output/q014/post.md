## Standalone
Bend, Oregon posted the fastest five-year per capita income growth among midsize-and-up US metros in this cut, up 48.1% from 2018 to 2023. San Jose, Boise, Provo, and Santa Cruz were close behind. The striking part is how many of the fastest growers are smaller Western metros rather than the biggest legacy hubs.
