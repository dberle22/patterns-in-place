## Standalone
Median gross rent rose across all 10 largest US metros after 2018, but the pace was not even. By 2023, Los Angeles was just above $2,000, Washington was near $1,980, and New York was around $1,853, while several large Sun Belt metros saw especially sharp post-2021 jumps. The shared direction is obvious, but the spread between metros is still huge.
