# Metro Deep Dive audience

**Date:** 2026-06
**Source:** `DEEP_DIVE_EXPLORATION.md`, Part 1

## Decision

The primary reader is the curious researcher or data nerd: someone interested in cities and data who wants the structural patterns under a place. Investors and people deciding where to live are secondary readers.

## Why

It's the reader Dan is, which makes it the cleanest audience to write for. Investors are better served by specific sections (the local economy). People choosing where to live rarely start from a full deep dive; comparative pieces such as "best places to retire" serve them better.

## What it means

- Lead with structure and surprise, not headline stats.
- Serve secondary readers through specific sections or separate formats, not by diluting the main report.
