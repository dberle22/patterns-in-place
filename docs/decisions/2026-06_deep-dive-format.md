# Metro Deep Dive format: long-form Substack first

**Date:** 2026-06
**Partly superseded by:** [the New York series](2026-09_ny-series-first.md): still long-form Substack, but a multi-part series rather than one market serialized by act.
**Source:** `docs/archive/2026-09_deep_dive_exploration/DEEP_DIVE_EXPLORATION.md`, Part 1; `docs/strategy/PUBLISHING.md`

## Decision

The first Metro Deep Dive is a long-form Substack post, serialized act by act (decided July 14). PDF reports and interactive Streamlit reports are longer-term options, not the starting point.

## Why

Long form leaves room to test different sections and formats. Changing the format later is cheaper than changing the method. A PDF or interactive report is more effort and more rigid than the work needs at this stage; short posts belong to the chart-a-day stream.

## What it means

- Each act is a standalone post that adds up to the full issue.
- Revisit the format after the first market is published.
