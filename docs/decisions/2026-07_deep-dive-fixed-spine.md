# Metro Deep Dive is the flagship, with a fixed spine

**Date:** 2026-07-11
**Source:** `metro-deep-dive/metro_deep_dive_build_approach.md`

## Decision

The Metro Deep Dive is the flagship series. Every issue shares a fixed spine; each market adapts only the flex sections. Published issues are dated artifacts and are not backfilled when new methods land.

## Why

A shared structure is what makes this a series rather than a pile of one-off city reports. Readers build pattern recognition across issues, benchmarks need a stable baseline, and market #3 should build on market #1.

## What it means

- Fixed spine: market verdict, fingerprint radar with locked axis order and percentile table, LQ quadrant and exposure scorecard, benchmarked zone composition, corridor stat blocks.
- Intelligence frames are analytical lenses, not report sections.
- Reuse over rebuild: new capability is built upstream as a reusable engine.
