# First Metro Deep Dive market: Richmond

**Date:** 2026-07
**Superseded by:** [First Metro Deep Dive work: the New York series](2026-09_ny-series-first.md). Richmond is paused and moves later.
**Source:** `docs/strategy/PUBLISHING.md` (from the July 14 publishing plan); `metro-deep-dive-program/metro_deep_dive_program.md` §7

## Decision

Richmond, VA is the first Metro Deep Dive market. Market-specific work lives in `metro-deep-dive-program/issues/richmond_va/`.

This replaces the June plan (Jacksonville first, Richmond as a companion). That plan was never recorded as a decision.

## Why

The first market has to both stress-test the template and be interesting to read (from the June answer). Richmond felt like a neutral city to write about, and Dan had visited twice on short trips. Richmond and Jacksonville are the two Phase 7 test markets.

## What it means

- Jacksonville work is reference material, not the first issue.
- Acts 1–3 can ship without zones or POI; Act 4 can wait for market 2 if its methods aren't ready.
