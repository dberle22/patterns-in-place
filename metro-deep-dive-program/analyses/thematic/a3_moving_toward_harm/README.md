# A3 — Are Americans Moving Toward Harm?

This folder will hold the national hazard-and-growth analysis and its reusable,
parameterized CBSA deep dive.

**Status:** Provisional scaffold. National hazard and growth inputs exist, but
their vintages and the meaning of “moving” require audit.

Start with:

- [THEMATIC_A3_MOVING_TOWARD_HARM_SPEC.md](THEMATIC_A3_MOVING_TOWARD_HARM_SPEC.md)
- [THEMATIC_A3_MOVING_TOWARD_HARM_BUILD_PLAN.md](THEMATIC_A3_MOVING_TOWARD_HARM_BUILD_PLAN.md)
- [../THEMATIC_ANALYSES_PLAN.md](../THEMATIC_ANALYSES_PLAN.md)

The national notebook will test whether population and housing growth are
concentrating in more hazard-exposed metros. The market notebook will show how
growth and hazard exposure overlap inside a selected CBSA. Neither notebook
should infer origin-to-destination movement without flow data.
