# A1 — The AI Inversion

This folder will hold the national AI Inversion analysis and its reusable,
parameterized CBSA deep dive.

**Status:** Provisional scaffold. Substantial prior work exists under
`metro-deep-dive/analysis_program/01_ai_inversion/`; nothing has been migrated
or superseded yet.

Start with:

- [THEMATIC_A1_AI_INVERSION_SPEC.md](THEMATIC_A1_AI_INVERSION_SPEC.md)
- [THEMATIC_A1_AI_INVERSION_BUILD_PLAN.md](THEMATIC_A1_AI_INVERSION_BUILD_PLAN.md)
- [../THEMATIC_ANALYSES_PLAN.md](../THEMATIC_ANALYSES_PLAN.md)

The national notebook will test how AI task exposure varies across metros. The
market notebook will decompose a selected CBSA’s exposure by occupation and
industry and place it in national, regional, and peer context. An actual
Richmond story notebook belongs under `issues/richmond_va/`.
