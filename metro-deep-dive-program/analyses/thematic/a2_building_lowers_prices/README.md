# A2 — Does Building Actually Lower Prices?

This folder will hold the national housing-supply/price analysis and its
reusable, parameterized CBSA deep dive.

**Status:** Provisional scaffold. Strong housing inputs and Q1 prior art exist;
the longitudinal research design is not yet settled.

Start with:

- [THEMATIC_A2_BUILDING_LOWERS_PRICES_SPEC.md](THEMATIC_A2_BUILDING_LOWERS_PRICES_SPEC.md)
- [THEMATIC_A2_BUILDING_LOWERS_PRICES_BUILD_PLAN.md](THEMATIC_A2_BUILDING_LOWERS_PRICES_BUILD_PLAN.md)
- [../THEMATIC_ANALYSES_PLAN.md](../THEMATIC_ANALYSES_PLAN.md)

The national notebook will test the cross-metro relationship between housing
production and later price/rent outcomes. The market notebook will show one
CBSA’s supply response, demand context, price path, and within-market evidence
without claiming that its local trend is causal.
