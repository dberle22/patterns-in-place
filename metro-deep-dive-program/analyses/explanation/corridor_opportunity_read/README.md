# Corridor Opportunity Read

**Build order:** E8 — closing synthesis; runs last

**Status:** Spec and build plan drafted; not startable until Q2, Q3, and Q4 produce output.

**Question:** Taken together, what do the preceding analyses say about which
corridors in this market deserve deeper attention, and why?

Purpose:

- synthesize the corridor-shaped results that emerge from Q2, Q3, and Q4
- shortlist and interpret corridors worth deeper issue attention
- hand candidate theses to the issue layer

**Primary analytical unit:** a corridor-shaped area of interest identified by the
upstream questions, within one selected market.

**National posture:** market scoped.

## Reframed: no engine dependency

This is **no longer a standalone analysis sitting on top of a Corridor
Intelligence engine.** That engine is a paused prototype and has been dropped as
a dependency.

Corridors now emerge organically from the 15-minute-city work — the access spine
in Q2, Q3, and Q4 keeps producing corridor-shaped results. This analysis
summarizes those. Jacksonville and Richmond Corridor Intelligence artifacts are
**method reference only**, not a candidate source.

Because of this, the previous Wave 3 gate — engine calibration, Richmond
validation, and a consumer handoff — no longer applies.

## V0 method

Synthesize across three evidence families:

1. structural role and connectivity
2. people, jobs, access, and housing conditions
3. current trajectory and issue relevance

## Guardrails

- do not create a canonical corridor boundary
- do not build one universal Investment Score before individual evidence families
  have been tested
- do not draw an investment conclusion from a corridor read

## Open decisions for the spec

- **how a corridor is identified from upstream outputs**, now that no engine
  supplies membership — the central open question
- comparison dimensions and evidence normalization
- selection versus ranking
- treatment of no-opportunity results
- handoff to issue-owned naming and stat blocks
