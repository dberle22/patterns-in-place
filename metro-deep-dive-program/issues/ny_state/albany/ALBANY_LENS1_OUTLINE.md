## The Argument

Albany is Upstate New York's best-off metro, built from four old cities around a state capital. It is the third-largest metro Upstate and the strongest per person, but demographically it looks like the rest of Upstate: older, mostly white, and growing slowly. It isn't one city with suburbs. Albany, Troy, Schenectady and Saratoga Springs each kept their own center, and the biggest suburb, the town of Colonie, has more people than any of them but Albany.

## 3. The Metro on the Map

- Five counties: Albany, Rensselaer and Schenectady are **central**; Saratoga and Schoharie are **outlying** (OMB 2023)
- Saratoga County is the fastest-growing (+8.4%, ACS 2008–12 to 2020–24) yet outlying; the Northway corridor (Clifton Park, Halfmoon, Malta) ties it to the core. Schoharie (−7.9%) is rural and the loosest member
- **Does every county belong** (high level only; the full commuting analysis is banked for Lens 2): Saratoga residents hold 39% of their jobs at home and 35% in the core; Schoharie sends 16% to the core, short of OMB's 25% rule on this data (state the threshold and that it's a LODES rerun, R12). One line on reach: Albany pulls workers from a ring of six outside counties
- **Top places:** towns, not Census places, are NY's suburbs. The town of Colonie (86k) is the second-largest municipality, larger than Schenectady (69k) or Troy (51k); Clifton Park 38k, Guilderland 37k and Bethlehem 35k are each larger than Saratoga Springs (29k). The suburbs people name (Latham, Loudonville, Delmar) are unincorporated hamlets inside these towns
- Each city is anchored by a college: UAlbany, RPI, Union, Skidmore

## 5. How It's Assembled

- Four cities, no center: Albany city has 11% of the metro's people. Job centers on all jobs: downtown Albany (caveat the 75+ mile workers) and the uptown-to-Wolf-Road band (96k jobs) as the shared center; Schenectady and Saratoga Springs run their own sheds
- Jobs against resident workers, not residents (R4)
- Zones (Dan's read, 2026-10-24): lower-income urban tracts in the hearts of Albany, Troy and Schenectady; most of the metro inner suburb, ringed by exurban and rural; Saratoga Springs suburban and more affluent; a large suburb in the middle of the triangle (Colonie) and continuing north (Clifton Park)
- Poverty is concentrated but not only urban: Albany city has 21% of the metro's residents in poverty with 11% of its people; rates are higher in Troy, Schenectady and Cohoes; 27% of the poor live in towns outside any Census place
