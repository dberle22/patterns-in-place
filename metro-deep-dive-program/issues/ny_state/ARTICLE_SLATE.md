# NY State of Mind: Article Slate

**Status:** Draft
**Updated:** 2026-10-20

What we plan to write after the Series Intro, and in roughly what order. Articles are assembled from question IDs and exhibits in the [repo context](upstate_downstate_repo_context.md) (E5). The order is flexible: it's a plan, not a commitment.

## Tracks

| Track | What it is | Size |
|---|---|---|
| **Metro spotlights** | One installment per lens for the larger metros, starting with Albany (5-county CBSA, 10580) | Full article or a short note, depending on what the lens turns up |
| **Statewide lens** | One statewide article per lens, each testing a finding from the Albany installments across the whole state | Full article |
| **Regional** | Opinionated pieces about where the lines fall | Short to medium |
| **Rankings** | Short ranked pieces with one or two exhibits | Short |

Larger metros, such as NYC and Buffalo, get all five lenses; not every metro needs them. Albany goes first because it's on the larger side and Dan knows it well, which makes it a good place to settle the format. Smaller metros may get one or two pieces built around their spotlight question (M.16, M.17, M.18).
