## v2026.1

**Status:** published September 28, 2026.

- **Zenodo record:** [zenodo.org/records/23020706](https://zenodo.org/records/23020706)
- **Version DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23020706](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23020706)
- **Concept DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23020705](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23020705)

This first version contains:

- OMB 2023 static county-to-CBSA crosswalk for all 935 current CBSAs.
- 2012–2024 ACS employment-composition and affordability measures.
- 2012–2024 county-derived BEA real GDP total and three optional industry shares.
- FY2012–FY2024 county-derived HUD two-bedroom FMR and FMR gap.

The immutable release contains three ZSTD Parquet files, one bundled DuckDB file,
`manifest.json`, `coverage_report.md`, and `CITATION.cff`. The manifest records artifact hashes
and release-specific provenance.
