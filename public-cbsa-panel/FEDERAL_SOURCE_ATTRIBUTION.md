# Federal source attribution

This release incorporates public information from the US Census Bureau, the Bureau of Economic Analysis, the Department of Housing and Urban Development, and Office of Management and Budget delineation materials. Patterns in Place is responsible for the county-to-CBSA processing and released derived tables; the agencies do not endorse this release or its interpretations.

Users should cite the release itself and, where their work depends on a particular source family, cite the relevant agency source as well. The source-family links and upstream variables are recorded in [audit/source_references.md](audit/source_references.md) and the table references in [`docs/`](docs/).
