# v2026.1 Public Release Scope

## Purpose

v2026.1 is the first release of **Patterns in Place: Metro & Micro Panel** (`pip-metro-micro-panel`), a narrow public release of Patterns in Place geographic harmonization and CBSA-year measures. It prioritizes a dependable, citable contract over breadth.

## Tables and grains

| Table | Grain | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cbsa_county_crosswalk` | One row per current county-to-CBSA membership | Static current-vintage county/state/CBSA identity, county role, CBSA type, and primary state. |
| `economics_industry_wide` | One row per CBSA-year | ACS employment composition and BEA industry-GDP measures. |
| `affordability_wide` | One row per CBSA-year | ACS housing and income measures, BEA regional price parity measures, and HUD two-bedroom FMR measures. |

The geographic universe is all 935 current CBSAs, including metropolitan and micropolitan areas. The crosswalk has no year field and is not a historical membership series.

## Candidate public allowlists

The lineage audit must confirm every field and its upstream variable before `config/tables.yml` becomes authoritative.

### `cbsa_county_crosswalk`

- County GEOID and name.
- State FIPS, abbreviation, and name.
- CBSA code, name, and type.
- A short `metro`/`micro` type label.
- County role: central/core or outlying.
- Deterministic primary state: the first state suffix in the official CBSA name.

### `economics_industry_wide`

- Standard fact-table geography fields: `geo_level`, `geo_id`, `geo_name`, `year`.
- ACS industry employment total, four broad industry shares, and ACS industry concentration HHI.
- County-derived BEA real GDP total and three broad industry shares. BEA GDP industry
  concentration HHI is deferred because its conservative-suppression coverage is too sparse.

### `affordability_wide`

- Standard fact-table geography fields: `geo_level`, `geo_id`, `geo_name`, `year`.
- ACS median gross rent, annualized median rent, median home value, median household income, rent-to-income, and value-to-income.
- HUD two-bedroom fair-market rent and its gap relative to ACS median gross rent.

## Exclusions

v2026.1 excludes Zillow/ZHVI/ZORI and all Zillow-derived values; BEA Regional Price Parities
and metro real-personal-income measures; BEA GDP concentration HHI; Census BFS and CBP; BLS
QCEW; permits; vacancy and occupancy; HUD CHAS burden measures; and all other Gold columns.
The included CBSA measures are derived from county-level source inputs and the static
county-to-CBSA crosswalk.

No excluded field may be added during implementation. A candidate field with incomplete or Zillow-tainted lineage is removed from v2026.1 rather than repaired by expanding scope.

## Documentation requirements

The public release must disclose the selected crosswalk vintage, source vintage and coverage for every public column, null behavior, derivation formulas, and whether the two fact tables are balanced by year. Different source vintages or coverage windows are allowed when plainly documented.

Release citation metadata will name **Dan Berle** as author and **Patterns in Place** as the
project brand.

## Acceptance criteria

- A lineage record exists for every published field, including upstream agency, dataset, variable, transformation, and source vintage.
- The crosswalk covers the agreed current CBSA universe and distinguishes central/core from outlying member counties where the source provides it.
- Export validation rejects undeclared columns and any Zillow lineage.
- A clean DuckDB installation can query the published Parquet files without credentials.
